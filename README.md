## 📌 Executive Summary

Unemployment rate serves as a vital barometer for economic stability and workforce health. This project conducts a comprehensive exploratory data analysis on regional employment statistics, examining historical spikes, labor force dynamics, and regional disparities—particularly highlighting disruptions across key socio-economic events.

---

## 🎯 Key Objectives

- 🔍 **Distribution Analysis:** Evaluate central tendencies, spreads, and outliers across estimated unemployment rates.
- 🗺️ **Geographic Disparities:** Pinpoint regions and states with the highest vs. lowest employment stability.
- ⏳ **Temporal Trend Mapping:** Chart longitudinal shifts to isolate peak unemployment surges across time.
- 🔗 **Correlation Evaluation:** Assess interplay between labor force participation rates, total employment counts, and unemployment metrics.
